Choco Boy — not at WinCo or Costco

Orion Choco Boy is a Korean snack, so neither store carries it. Your options: an Asian grocery in Provo or Orem (call ahead to check), or order online now from Weee! or Walmart.com — the Walmart listing is a third-party seller, and neither is guaranteed by Friday. If you can't find it, Pocky is the easy stand-in: same chocolate-dipped biscuit idea, and far more widely stocked.

Picnic food safety

Turkey, cheese, and anything with mayo or ranch go in a cooler with ice packs. Out of the cooler, the usual rule is about two hours before they should go back in or get tossed.

Smith's — only if you're passing by

No longer a regular stop. Worth it for two things: Hillshire Farm Turkey Sausage Patties if the Jimmy Dean links from Costco don't work for you (130 cal, 13g protein per 2), and weekly-ad fruit deals — last week had Honeycrisp apples at $2/lb and a 5-lb bag of mandarins for $5. The ad resets every Wednesday.

One-time gear

PackIt Freezable Lunch Bag — the whole bag freezes, so there's no separate ice pack to forget. Street price runs about $20–24: Walmart ~$19.99 (verify the seller is Walmart, not a marketplace reseller) or Amazon around $23.99. Not stocked at Costco, WinCo or Smith's — order it so it lands before a Wednesday.

Picnic gear — reference only

Not on the checklist and not counted above. Just a starting pick for each, to compare against your own research.

Thermos: Thermos Stainless King beverage bottle, 24 oz. GearJunkie named it the best coffee thermos in the Thermos line at under $40, and it keeps drinks hot all day. 24 oz is plenty for two. Walmart (sold by Thermos directly, not Walmart).

Soft cooler: Arctic Zone Titan Deep Freeze, a small size. OutdoorGearLab's pick under $100 for a day out. Pack Hacker found it weak over 24 hours, which doesn't matter for a picnic. Sold in stores, so you can get it this week. If you want something lighter that folds flat, REI Co-op Campwell 18L was Switchback's budget day-trip pick.

Blanket: anything with a water-resistant backing so damp grass doesn't soak through, and machine washable. Not researched here.

4 reusable water bottles: a store multipack is the budget move; nothing here needs to be insulated for an afternoon. Not researched here.

Foil or plastic wrap: store brand, and WinCo has it — grab it on the regular run.

Lidded snack-box container: any leak-resistant container with a snap lid; a divided one keeps the crackers away from the grapes. Not researched here.

Small knife and napkins: a paring knife with a blade cover for the apples and cheese, and store-brand napkins. Not researched here.
